## `client.BillingPortal.EnableBillingPortalForCustomer` — `POST /portal/customers/{customer_id}/enable.json` (`enableBillingPortalForCustomer`)

- **loud**, state/errors: Billing Portal access is enabled for a customer who already has it enabled → 422 "Portal is already enabled for this customer.". **Do:** Check portal_customer_created_at or handle the 422 as already enabled instead of retrying. *(spec `spec/openapi.yaml`; `maxio-c4:enableBillingPortalForCustomer.1`)*
- **silent**, -/side-effects: auto_invite=1 is sent in the query when enabling the Billing Portal → An invitation email is sent to the customer; with auto_invite=0 or no auto_invite, no email is sent.. **Do:** Send auto_invite=1 only when the customer should receive the portal invitation email now. *(spec `spec/openapi.yaml`; `maxio-c4:enableBillingPortalForCustomer.2`)*

## `client.BillingPortal.ReadBillingPortalLink` — `GET /portal/customers/{customer_id}/management_link.json` (`readBillingPortalLink`)
