- **loud**, state/errors: An advance invoice is requested for a subscription that has nothing due → 422 "Cannot generate an invoice in advance when nothing is due". **Do:** Check that the subscription has an amount due at its next renewal before issuing an advance invoice. *(spec `spec/openapi.yaml`; `maxio-c4:issueAdvanceInvoice.1`)*
- **loud**, state/errors: An advance invoice is requested for a subscription that uses prepaid components → 422 "Cannot generate an invoice in advance when a subscription uses prepaid components". **Do:** Do not offer advance invoices for subscriptions with prepaid components. *(spec `spec/openapi.yaml`; `maxio-c4:issueAdvanceInvoice.10`)*

- **loud**, state/errors: An advance invoice is requested for an inactive (end-of-life) subscription → 422 "Cannot generate an invoice in advance for inactive subscription". **Do:** Issue advance invoices only for live subscriptions. *(spec `spec/openapi.yaml`; `maxio-c4:issueAdvanceInvoice.5`)*
- **loud**, state/errors: An advance invoice is requested for a subscription on calendar billing → 422 "Cannot generate an invoice in advance for a calendar billing subscription". **Do:** Do not offer advance invoices for calendar-billing subscriptions. *(spec `spec/openapi.yaml`; `maxio-c4:issueAdvanceInvoice.6`)*
