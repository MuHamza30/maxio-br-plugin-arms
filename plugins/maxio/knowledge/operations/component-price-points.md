- **loud**, -/errors: Price brackets overlap → 422 "There cannot be any overlapping bracket ranges". **Do:** Make price bracket ranges non-overlapping. Also: `updateComponentPricePoint`. *(spec `spec/openapi.yaml`; `maxio-c4:createComponentPricePoint.1`)*
- **loud**, -/errors: There is a quantity gap between successive price brackets → 422 "Successive brackets may not have gaps in quantity (i.e. if one bracket ends at quantity 10, the next must begin at quantity 11)". **Do:** Start each bracket at the previous bracket's ending_quantity plus 1.
