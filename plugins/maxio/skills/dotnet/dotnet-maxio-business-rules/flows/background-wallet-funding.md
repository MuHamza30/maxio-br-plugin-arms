# Background: wallet-funding

- **silent**, -/values (`background:wallet-funding`): The entitlement allowance behind a recurring wallet funding is lowered → Nothing is clawed back; the lower allowance applies from the next scheduled refill.. **Do:** Expect a lowered allowance to show only at the next refill. *(`maxio-c4:background.76`)*
- **silent**, -/side-effects (`background:wallet-funding`): The entitlement allowance behind a recurring wallet funding is raised mid-period → The difference is granted immediately as a grant with origin allowance_increase.. **Do:** Expect an immediate allowance_increase grant after raising an allowance. *(`maxio-c4:background.77`)*
- **silent**, -/values (`background:wallet-funding`): Recurring wallet funding starts for a subscription → The first grant lands immediately for the period in progress; refills then follow the entitlement's own interval, independent of the billing term (the first refill one interval after the period start, or at the period end when the interval matches the billing cadence or the period is a trial or prorated).. **Do:** Compute expected refill dates from the entitlement's periodicity, not the billing period. *(`maxio-c4:background.78`)*
- **silent**, order/side-effects (`background:wallet-funding`, `background:renewal`): A wallet refill is due at a billing period boundary → The refill waits for the subscription's renewal to run first, so a delayed cancellation or product change is applied before anything is granted.. **Do:** Expect boundary refills after the renewal has been processed. *(`maxio-c4:background.79`)*
- **silent**, state/side-effects (`background:wallet-funding`): A recurring funding grant cannot be written (status erroring) → The job keeps retrying; for allowance_not_representable fixing the entitlement resumes funding, and for balance_at_capacity the wallet balance must come down first.. **Do:** Watch recurring_funding status and reason, and fix the entitlement or lower the balance. *(`maxio-c4:background.80`)*
- **silent**, state/- (`background:wallet-funding`): Recurring funding has status stopped → Funding has ended for good; units already granted stay spendable.. **Do:** Do not expect more grants from a stopped schedule. *(`maxio-c4:background.81`)*
- **silent**, state/side-effects (`background:wallet-funding`): The subscription behind recurring wallet funding becomes canceled, expired or trial_ended → The funding schedule stops for good.. **Do:** Do not expect refills after the subscription is canceled, expired or its trial ended. *(`maxio-c4:background.82`)*
- **silent**, state/side-effects (`background:wallet-funding`): The subscription behind recurring wallet funding is trialing → Refills are retried rather than granted, even though trialing subscriptions otherwise bill normally.. **Do:** Do not expect refills during a trial. *(`maxio-c4:background.83`)*
- **silent**, state/side-effects (`background:wallet-funding`): The subscription behind recurring wallet funding is in another non-live state (on_hold, paused and the like) or in past_due, soft_failure or unpaid → Other non-live states skip that period without stopping the schedule; past_due, soft_failure and unpaid still grant normally.. **Do:** Expect skipped refills while a subscription is on hold or paused. *(`maxio-c4:background.84`)*
- **silent**, -/values (`background:wallet-funding`): A funding period is skipped because the subscription was not live, or a scheduled run was late (for example during an outage) → skipped_periods counts both kinds of gap.. **Do:** Do not read skipped_periods as only non-live periods. *(`maxio-c4:background.85`)*
- **silent**, state/side-effects (`background:wallet-funding`, `client.Wallets.ArchiveWallet`): A wallet with recurring funding is archived → Recurring funding stops with reason wallet_archived.. **Do:** Do not archive a wallet that should keep receiving refills. *(`maxio-c4:background.87`)*
