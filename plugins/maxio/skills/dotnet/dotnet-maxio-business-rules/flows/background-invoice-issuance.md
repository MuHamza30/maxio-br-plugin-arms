# Background: invoice-issuance

- **silent**, state/side-effects (`background:invoice-issuance`): An invoice is issued while the subscription has prepayments → Prepayments are applied automatically only when the invoice has no net terms, whatever the collection method. **Do:** Apply prepayments yourself to invoices with net terms when they should be used. *(`maxio-c4:background.61`)*
- **silent**, account-config/side-effects (`background:invoice-issuance`): An invoice is issued for an automatic-collection subscription and the site's prepayment setting is left at its default → Prepayments are applied automatically on the invoice due date (other options: never automatically, or immediately on issuance; set under Config > Settings > Prepayments). **Do:** Check the site's prepayment setting before assuming when prepayments are applied. *(`maxio-c4:background.62`)*
- **silent**, account-config/side-effects (`background:invoice-issuance`): An invoice is issued for a remittance subscription and the site's prepayment setting is left at its default → Available prepayments are applied automatically immediately on issuance (the other option is not to apply them automatically). **Do:** Check the site's prepayment setting before assuming when prepayments are applied. *(`maxio-c4:background.63`)*
