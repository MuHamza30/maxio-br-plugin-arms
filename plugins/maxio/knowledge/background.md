# Rules for jobs Maxio runs on its own

## dunning

- **silent**, -/webhooks: A subscription reaches a step of the dunning process → dunning_step_reached fires.. **Do:** Subscribe to dunning_step_reached to follow dunning progress. *(docs `content/introduction/webhooks/webhooks-reference.md`; `maxio-c4:background.27`)*
- **silent**, state/webhooks: A subscription is in dunning after its first dunning attempt → Renewal webhooks trigger only on the normal renewal dates, not daily.. **Do:** Do not treat dunning retries as renewals. *(announcement `content/help/announcements/2023-02-03-unintended-renewal-event-after-the-first-dunning-attempt.md`; `maxio-c4:background.43`)*
- **silent**, -/side-effects: A credit card transaction fails or a card is expiring → Advanced Billing runs the dunning process (customer communication per the site's dunning plan) itself.. **Do:** Configure dunning plans in Advanced Billing rather than building your own retry emails. *(docs `content/introduction/about-the-api/expert-usage.md`; docs `content/introduction/about-the-api/expert-usage.md`; `maxio-c4:background.70`)*

## entitlement-provisioning

- **silent**, order/side-effects: A feature is attached to a product or component that subscribers already have → Subscribers are provisioned the entitlement automatically only the next time their subscription changes (for example signup, plan change or component allocation).. **Do:** Do not expect existing subscribers to get a new feature until their subscription next changes. *(docs `content/introduction/basic-concepts/entitlements.md`; `maxio-c4:background.72`)*

## exports

- **silent**, order/other: An export is created → The export runs as an asynchronous batch job: the create call returns a batch job, whose status is read with the Read endpoint before the rows are listed.. **Do:** Poll the export's batch job with the Read endpoint and list rows only after it completes. *(docs `content/introduction/about-the-api/tips.md`; spec `spec/openapi.yaml`; `maxio-c4:background.73`)*

## invoice-issuance

- **silent**, state/side-effects: An invoice is issued while the subscription has prepayments → Prepayments are applied automatically only when the invoice has no net terms, whatever the collection method.. **Do:** Apply prepayments yourself to invoices with net terms when they should be used. **Conflict:** The 2023-01-18 announcement later made prepayment application a site setting with defaults per collection method; this record follows the 2022-11-03 text. *(announcement `content/help/announcements/2022-11-03-prepayments-no-longer-applied-automatically-for-remittance-invoice-with-net-terms.md`; `maxio-c4:background.61`)*
- **silent**, account-config/side-effects: An invoice is issued for an automatic-collection subscription and the site's prepayment setting is left at its default → Prepayments are applied automatically on the invoice due date (other options: never automatically, or immediately on issuance; set under Config > Settings > Prepayments).. **Do:** Check the site's prepayment setting before assuming when prepayments are applied. *(announcement `content/help/announcements/2023-01-18-ability-to-set-custom-settings-for-applying-prepayments-to-invoices.md`; announcement `content/help/announcements/2023-01-18-ability-to-set-custom-settings-for-applying-prepayments-to-invoices.md`; `maxio-c4:background.62`)*
- **silent**, account-config/side-effects: An invoice is issued for a remittance subscription and the site's prepayment setting is left at its default → Available prepayments are applied automatically immediately on issuance (the other option is not to apply them automatically).. **Do:** Check the site's prepayment setting before assuming when prepayments are applied. *(announcement `content/help/announcements/2023-01-18-ability-to-set-custom-settings-for-applying-prepayments-to-invoices.md`; `maxio-c4:background.63`)*

## invoicing

- **silent**, account-config/values: A hybrid price point is billed → The customer sees one invoice line item per billing period for the component, combining primary and secondary charges.. **Do:** Expect one combined line item, not separate primary and overage lines, for hybrid price points. *(docs `content/introduction/basic-concepts/hybrid-pricing.md`; `maxio-c4:background.71`)*

## payments

- **silent**, account-config/values: The site's payment gateway is Authorize.Net → Payment webhooks carry an extra gateway_response with approval_code.. **Do:** Treat gateway_response as optional and gateway-specific. *(docs `content/introduction/webhooks/webhooks-reference.md`; `maxio-c4:background.35`)*

## renewal

- **silent**, -/webhooks: A metered component's unit balance is reset to 0 at renewal → metered_usage does not fire for the reset; it fires for reported usage, with timestamp in UTC.. **Do:** Do not expect a metered_usage webhook at each renewal reset. *(docs `content/introduction/webhooks/webhooks-reference.md`; `maxio-c4:background.32`)*
- **silent**, -/webhooks: A recurring interval ends for a subscription → Exactly one renewal_success or renewal_failure fires.. **Do:** Expect one renewal event per interval. *(docs `content/introduction/webhooks/webhooks-reference.md`; `maxio-c4:background.40`)*
- **silent**, state/webhooks: A renewal failed and a later payment brings the account current → No renewal_success fires; payment_success and subscription_state_change fire instead.. **Do:** Detect recovery after renewal_failure from payment_success and subscription_state_change. *(docs `content/introduction/webhooks/webhooks-reference.md`; `maxio-c4:background.41`)*
- **silent**, state/webhooks: A subscription renews while it is past due → Renewal webhooks are still triggered.. **Do:** Handle renewal webhooks for past-due subscriptions. *(announcement `content/help/announcements/2023-01-24-renewal-success-and-renewal-failure-events-changes.md`; `maxio-c4:background.42`)*
- **silent**, state/webhooks: A subscription is in dunning after its first dunning attempt → Renewal webhooks trigger only on the normal renewal dates, not daily.. **Do:** Do not treat dunning retries as renewals. *(announcement `content/help/announcements/2023-02-03-unintended-renewal-event-after-the-first-dunning-attempt.md`; `maxio-c4:background.43`)*
- **silent**, -/webhooks: A subscription is 3 days from renewing → upcoming_renewal_notice fires.. **Do:** Use upcoming_renewal_notice for pre-renewal reminders. *(docs `content/introduction/webhooks/webhooks-reference.md`; `maxio-c4:background.55`)*
- **silent**, -/webhooks: A statement closes at the end of a period and payment was received or not required → statement_settled fires.. **Do:** Treat statement_settled as the paid outcome of a period. *(docs `content/introduction/webhooks/webhooks-reference.md`; `maxio-c4:background.57`)*
- **silent**, -/webhooks: A statement closes at the end of a period without successful payment → statement_closed fires, and statement_settled may follow later if the statement becomes paid (for example after a dunning retry or card update).. **Do:** Keep statement state open after statement_closed and update it on a later statement_settled. *(docs `content/introduction/webhooks/webhooks-reference.md`; `maxio-c4:background.58`)*
- **silent**, state/side-effects: A subscription with automatic payment collection renews → The customer is charged automatically.. **Do:** Keep a valid payment method on automatic subscriptions before renewal. *(docs `content/introduction/basic-concepts/subscription-signup.md`; `maxio-c4:background.59`)*
- **silent**, state/side-effects: A subscription with remittance collection renews → The customer is not charged; an invoice is generated, and payment is recorded manually once received.. **Do:** Record remittance payments yourself when they arrive. *(docs `content/introduction/basic-concepts/subscription-signup.md`; `maxio-c4:background.60`)*
- **silent**, -/side-effects: A subscription group renews → Payment success and failure events are created for the primary subscription, as at signup.. **Do:** Track group renewal payments through the primary subscription's events. *(announcement `content/help/announcements/2022-11-21-create-successful-and-failure-payment-events-for-subscription-group-renewal.md`; `maxio-c4:background.64`)*
- **silent**, -/values: A billing period ends for a subscription with metered and quantity-based components → Metered component usage resets to 0; quantity-based allocations do not reset.. **Do:** Report metered usage per period and do not re-allocate quantity components each period. *(spec `spec/openapi.yaml`; spec `spec/openapi.yaml`; docs `content/introduction/basic-concepts/product-catalog.md`; `maxio-c4:background.65`)*
- **silent**, state/values: A subscription renews with an event-based component → Event usage is billed only if the component is activated for the subscription; events of a deactivated component are ignored at renewal, although events can be streamed at any time.. **Do:** Activate the event-based component for each subscription that should be billed for its events. *(spec `spec/openapi.yaml`; spec `spec/openapi.yaml`; `maxio-c4:background.66`)*
- **silent**, order/side-effects: A delayed product change (product_change_delayed=true) is set on a subscription → The product change happens automatically at the next renewal date, with no proration; setting next_product_id to an empty string cancels it.. **Do:** Expect the new product only from the next renewal and cancel with an empty next_product_id if needed. *(spec `spec/openapi.yaml`; spec `spec/openapi.yaml`; `maxio-c4:background.67`)*
- **silent**, order/side-effects: A delayed cancellation is set on a subscription → The subscription is canceled at the end of the current billing period, unless the delayed cancellation is removed first.. **Do:** Remove the delayed cancellation before the period ends if the customer stays. *(spec `spec/openapi.yaml`; spec `spec/openapi.yaml`; docs `content/introduction/basic-concepts/subscription-signup.md`; `maxio-c4:background.68`)*
- **silent**, state/side-effects: A subscription is on hold → It does not renew, and it resumes automatically on automatically_resume_at when that date is set.. **Do:** Set or clear automatically_resume_at to control when a held subscription resumes. *(spec `spec/openapi.yaml`; spec `spec/openapi.yaml`; `maxio-c4:background.69`)*
- **silent**, order/side-effects: A wallet refill is due at a billing period boundary → The refill waits for the subscription's renewal to run first, so a delayed cancellation or product change is applied before anything is granted.. **Do:** Expect boundary refills after the renewal has been processed. *(docs `content/introduction/basic-concepts/wallets.md`; `maxio-c4:background.79`)*

## wallet-funding

- **silent**, -/values: The entitlement allowance behind a recurring wallet funding is lowered → Nothing is clawed back; the lower allowance applies from the next scheduled refill.. **Do:** Expect a lowered allowance to show only at the next refill. *(docs `content/introduction/basic-concepts/wallets.md`; `maxio-c4:background.76`)*
- **silent**, -/side-effects: The entitlement allowance behind a recurring wallet funding is raised mid-period → The difference is granted immediately as a grant with origin allowance_increase.. **Do:** Expect an immediate allowance_increase grant after raising an allowance. *(docs `content/introduction/basic-concepts/wallets.md`; `maxio-c4:background.77`)*
- **silent**, -/values: Recurring wallet funding starts for a subscription → The first grant lands immediately for the period in progress; refills then follow the entitlement's own interval, independent of the billing term (the first refill one interval after the period start, or at the period end when the interval matches the billing cadence or the period is a trial or prorated).. **Do:** Compute expected refill dates from the entitlement's periodicity, not the billing period. *(docs `content/introduction/basic-concepts/wallets.md`; docs `content/introduction/basic-concepts/wallets.md`; `maxio-c4:background.78`)*
- **silent**, order/side-effects: A wallet refill is due at a billing period boundary → The refill waits for the subscription's renewal to run first, so a delayed cancellation or product change is applied before anything is granted.. **Do:** Expect boundary refills after the renewal has been processed. *(docs `content/introduction/basic-concepts/wallets.md`; `maxio-c4:background.79`)*
- **silent**, state/side-effects: A recurring funding grant cannot be written (status erroring) → The job keeps retrying; for allowance_not_representable fixing the entitlement resumes funding, and for balance_at_capacity the wallet balance must come down first.. **Do:** Watch recurring_funding status and reason, and fix the entitlement or lower the balance. *(docs `content/introduction/basic-concepts/wallets.md`; docs `content/introduction/basic-concepts/wallets.md`; `maxio-c4:background.80`)*
- **silent**, state/-: Recurring funding has status stopped → Funding has ended for good; units already granted stay spendable.. **Do:** Do not expect more grants from a stopped schedule. *(docs `content/introduction/basic-concepts/wallets.md`; `maxio-c4:background.81`)*
- **silent**, state/side-effects: The subscription behind recurring wallet funding becomes canceled, expired or trial_ended → The funding schedule stops for good.. **Do:** Do not expect refills after the subscription is canceled, expired or its trial ended. *(docs `content/introduction/basic-concepts/wallets.md`; `maxio-c4:background.82`)*
- **silent**, state/side-effects: The subscription behind recurring wallet funding is trialing → Refills are retried rather than granted, even though trialing subscriptions otherwise bill normally.. **Do:** Do not expect refills during a trial. *(docs `content/introduction/basic-concepts/wallets.md`; `maxio-c4:background.83`)*
- **silent**, state/side-effects: The subscription behind recurring wallet funding is in another non-live state (on_hold, paused and the like) or in past_due, soft_failure or unpaid → Other non-live states skip that period without stopping the schedule; past_due, soft_failure and unpaid still grant normally.. **Do:** Expect skipped refills while a subscription is on hold or paused. *(docs `content/introduction/basic-concepts/wallets.md`; `maxio-c4:background.84`)*
- **silent**, -/values: A funding period is skipped because the subscription was not live, or a scheduled run was late (for example during an outage) → skipped_periods counts both kinds of gap.. **Do:** Do not read skipped_periods as only non-live periods. *(docs `content/introduction/basic-concepts/wallets.md`; `maxio-c4:background.85`)*
- **silent**, state/side-effects: A wallet with recurring funding is archived → Recurring funding stops with reason wallet_archived.. **Do:** Do not archive a wallet that should keep receiving refills. *(docs `content/introduction/basic-concepts/wallets.md`; `maxio-c4:background.87`)*

## wallet-grant-expiration

- **silent**, state/side-effects: A wallet grant reaches its expires_at before being fully spent → The remainder is written off automatically as a kind expiration ledger row, and the grant's expired_at/expired_on are filled.. **Do:** Expect expiration rows and a lower balance when grants expire. *(docs `content/introduction/basic-concepts/wallets.md`; `maxio-c4:background.86`)*

## wallet-provisioning

- **silent**, account-config/side-effects: A Feature has wallet_behavior and a subscription resolves an entitlement to it → A wallet with origin feature is provisioned automatically; Create Wallet is never called for it.. **Do:** Configure wallet_behavior on the Feature template instead of creating these wallets yourself. *(docs `content/introduction/basic-concepts/wallets.md`; `maxio-c4:background.74`)*
- **silent**, state/side-effects: The subscription that resolves a wallet-backed entitlement is still pending or awaiting signup → No wallet is provisioned until the subscription goes live.. **Do:** Expect the feature wallet only after the subscription is live. *(docs `content/introduction/basic-concepts/wallets.md`; `maxio-c4:background.75`)*

## webhook-delivery

- **silent**, resilience/webhooks: Advanced Billing posts a webhook and the endpoint answers with anything other than 200 OK, or does not answer within about 15 seconds → The webhook is not accepted and is retried automatically.. **Do:** Answer webhooks with 200 OK as fast as possible and do slow work after responding. *(docs `content/introduction/webhooks/webhooks.md`; spec `spec/components/schemas/Webhook.yaml`; `maxio-c4:background.1`)*
- **silent**, -/values: A webhook payload contains timestamps → Timestamps use the site's time zone (Eastern Time by default, with daylight saving), except newer events such as component_allocation_change and metered_usage, which use ISO 8601 in UTC.. **Do:** Parse webhook timestamps per event type and do not assume one time zone. *(docs `content/introduction/webhooks/webhooks.md`; docs `content/introduction/about-the-api/data-types.md`; `maxio-c4:background.10`)*
- **silent**, -/webhooks: One system event happens, such as creating a subscription that needs a payment → Several webhooks can fire for it (for example signup_success and payment_success).. **Do:** Handle related webhooks independently and in any order. *(docs `content/introduction/webhooks/webhooks-reference.md`; `maxio-c4:background.11`)*
- **silent**, -/values: A webhook payload is built from a resource → Not every value is populated; for example reason_code and component allocations are not included in signup_success.. **Do:** Treat payload fields as optional and read the resource through the API when a value is missing. *(docs `content/introduction/webhooks/webhooks-reference.md`; docs `content/introduction/webhooks/webhooks-reference.md`; `maxio-c4:background.12`)*
- **silent**, account-config/webhooks: The site uses the enhanced (new) Catalog experience → Webhook event names and messages change: subscription_product_change becomes subscription_plan_change, component_allocation_change becomes allocation_change, and component_billing_date_change becomes product_billing_date_change.. **Do:** Handle both the old and the new event names when the catalog experience may be enabled. **Conflict:** The 2026 announcement says the catalog changes are limited to the application UI; this record follows webhooks-reference.md, which lists renamed webhook events. *(docs `content/introduction/webhooks/webhooks-reference.md`; docs `content/introduction/webhooks/webhooks-reference.md`; `maxio-c4:background.13`)*
- **silent**, -/webhooks: An application relies on webhooks to react to events → Webhooks are asynchronous and may arrive late, so they are not suited to time-sensitive actions.. **Do:** Do not block signups or provisioning on webhooks; verify subscriptions through the API. *(docs `content/introduction/webhooks/webhooks.md`; docs `content/introduction/webhooks/webhooks.md`; `maxio-c4:background.14`)*
- **silent**, -/values: A test webhook is sent from the Webhooks Control Panel → The endpoint receives the payload id=123456&event=test&payload[chargify]=testing, which may not match production webhooks.. **Do:** Accept and ignore event=test webhooks without failing. *(docs `content/introduction/webhooks/webhooks.md`; docs `content/introduction/webhooks/webhooks.md`; `maxio-c4:background.15`)*
- **silent**, -/webhooks: A subscription's billing date changes → billing_date_change fires only for an explicit change through the application or API, not for a normal renewal, period advance or migration.. **Do:** Do not use billing_date_change to track renewals. *(docs `content/introduction/webhooks/webhooks-reference.md`; `maxio-c4:background.16`)*
- **silent**, -/webhooks: Component allocations are set during the initial subscription creation (signup) → component_allocation_change does not fire; it fires only for allocation, on/off or prepaid purchase changes made after signup.. **Do:** Read initial allocations from the subscription, not from component_allocation_change. *(docs `content/introduction/webhooks/webhooks-reference.md`; `maxio-c4:background.17`)*
- **silent**, -/values: component_allocation_change fires for an on/off component → previous_allocation and new_allocation are 0 (off) or 1 (on), and timestamp is ISO 8601 in UTC.. **Do:** Read on/off component changes as 0/1 values and parse the timestamp as UTC. *(docs `content/introduction/webhooks/webhooks-reference.md`; `maxio-c4:background.18`)*
